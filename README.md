## About

I'm a BBA (Computer Application) graduate from Abeda Inamdar Senior College, Pune (affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University), working as a full-stack developer and freelancer based in Pune, India.

I treat AI as an architectural partner rather than a shortcut — using a **Context-First Engineering** approach to design and ship production-ready web and mobile systems quickly without sacrificing quality. My background is hybrid: technical depth across Flutter, Kotlin, PHP, and Python, paired with hands-on experience running Google and Meta ad campaigns. That combination means I build products *and* understand how they'll actually be marketed and used.

**Currently open to full-stack roles where I can take ownership of real products end-to-end.**
